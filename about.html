We believe the deodorant aisle is fundamentally broken.

So we built a brand based on a very plain theory: skin science should be simple, honest, and for every body.

The Problems We Set Out to Solve

01

Harsh, Questionable Actives

For decades, the industry relied on aluminum chlorohydrate, parabens, and synthetic fragrances. These drive growing consumer distrust and can irritate sensitive underarm skin. We knew there was a better way.

02

Gendered by Default

'His' and 'Her' packaging splits one product into two SKUs, wrapped in pink or dark blue plastic, often charging women a "pink tax". Sweat is human. Deodorant should be too.

03

Short-lived & Over-priced

Aerosol sprays evaporate fast and choke the room. Most 'natural' roll-ons are so thin they don't survive a month of daily use. We formulated our roll-on to dispense perfectly, stretching 50ml to 6-8 weeks.

Works with the body, not against it.

Sweating is a natural, necessary function. It regulates your temperature and expels toxins. The problem isn't sweat—it's the bacteria that breaks sweat down.

Traditional antiperspirants plug your sweat glands with aluminum salts. We take a different approach. Our active ingredient, Potassium Alum, is a naturally occurring mineral that sits on the surface of the skin, creating a mildly acidic environment where odor-causing bacteria simply cannot survive.

No blocked pores. No stained shirts. Just clean, 24-hour freshness that lets your body do what it was designed to do.
