Why it Works
Most natural deodorants fail because they try to absorb sweat (which is impossible) or mask odor. We use Potassium Alum, a naturally occurring mineral salt that leaves an invisible, mildly acidic film on the skin. This prevents odor-causing bacteria from multiplying in the first place. You still sweat, but you don't smell.
Full Ingredients
Aqua (Water), Potassium Alum (17%), Glycerin (3%), Polyglyceryl-4 Caprate, Xanthan Gum, Rosemary Leaf Extract, Tocopherol, Essential Oils.

Free from: Aluminum chlorohydrate, parabens, phthalates, synthetic fragrances ('parfum'), baking soda, and synthetic dyes.
How to Use
Shake gently before use. Roll 2-3 swipes onto clean, dry underarms. Allow a moment to dry before dressing. Built thick to dispense slowly—one 50ml bottle lasts 6-8 weeks of daily use.
