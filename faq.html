Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our science, our formulas, and your skin.

How It Works

Will I still sweat while using this?
Yes! Sweating is a natural, healthy way for your body to cool down and release toxins. Our deodorant doesn't block your sweat glands with aluminum chlorohydrate. Instead, our active ingredient (Potassium Alum) creates an invisible shield that prevents odor-causing bacteria from surviving. You will sweat naturally, but you won't smell.
What exactly is Potassium Alum?
Potassium Alum is a naturally occurring mineral salt. Unlike synthetic aluminum compounds found in common antiperspirants—which physically plug your pores—Potassium Alum molecules are too large to be absorbed into your skin. It stays safely on the surface, keeping you fresh for up to 24 hours.
How long does one bottle last?
A typical 50ml natural roll-on runs out in less than a month because it dispenses too quickly. We fixed this by using Xanthan Gum, a natural thickener. This gives our formula the perfect texture so it rolls on smoothly and stretches one bottle to last 6 to 8 weeks of daily use.

Safety & Shelf Life

Is it safe for sensitive skin?
Yes. We formulated this specifically with sensitive skin in mind. We eliminated baking soda (a common irritant in natural deodorants), synthetic "parfum" (which hides hundreds of chemicals), and harsh preservatives like parabens. Plus, we added plant-derived glycerin to keep your underarms moisturized.
What is the shelf life?
Our roll-ons have a stable shelf life of 18 to 24 months. We achieve this naturally using a blend of Rosemary Leaf Extract and Vitamin E (Tocopherol)—completely eliminating the need for formaldehyde-releasing preservatives.
Why does it say "Unisex by Default"?
Because sweat doesn't have a gender. We don't believe in charging a "pink tax" for women or making overly aggressive branding for men. Our packaging is neutral, and our natural essential oil scents (like Eucalyptus & Rosemary or Sandalwood) smell incredible on absolutely everyone.

Skin Types & Care

Is it suitable for dry or easily irritated skin?
Absolutely. We purposely formulated this with a generous amount of Plant-Derived Glycerin (3%). Glycerin is a powerful humectant that draws moisture to the surface of your skin, preventing the dry, chalky feeling that many natural deodorants leave behind.
Will it cause underarm darkening or pigmentation?
Underarm darkening is commonly caused by friction, irritation from baking soda, or harsh synthetic alcohols drying out the skin. Our formula is completely free from baking soda and synthetic alcohols. The smooth roll-on application minimizes friction, helping to maintain your natural skin tone.
Can I use it if I'm a heavy sweater or have oily skin?
Yes! Remember, odor doesn't come from sweat itself; it comes from bacteria breaking down the sweat. By targeting the bacteria with antimicrobial Potassium Alum, heavy sweaters can enjoy all-day freshness. For best results, ensure your underarms are completely clean and dry before applying.
Can I apply it immediately after shaving or waxing?
Potassium Alum is traditionally used as a soothing aftershave to calm the skin and prevent bumps. However, because it's a mineral salt, it may cause a brief, mild tingling sensation if you have micro-cuts from a razor. We recommend waiting 10-15 minutes after shaving before application.
